# 46 — One `room_selector`, written by us: vanilla's rooms restored, STNH's Trek rooms reached

**Resolved 2026-08-08.** Closes the *rooms* half of plan.md §6 Phase 3's
"Outstanding" list.

## What was on disk

`gfx/portraits/asset_selectors/room_textures.txt` in the built tree was
**STNH's**, at vanilla's path, shadowing it outright. 121 lines against vanilla
4.4's 404. It is a 3.12-era file, and the arithmetic of what it dropped:

| | vanilla 4.4 | STNH's copy |
|---|---|---|
| `game_setup` rows (what the designer lists) | 42 | 19 |
| `ruler` rows (what an empire gets in play) | 55 | 41, of which 29 are STNH's own |

So **23 vanilla rooms could not be picked in the empire designer** — `machine_room`,
`lithoid_room`, `crystalline_room`, `necroids_room`, `aquatics_room`,
`toxoids_room`, `cybernetics_room`, `synthetics_room`, `psionic_room`,
`biogenesis_01/02`, `runic`, `scrappy`, `organic`, `gilded`, `solarpunk`,
`ethic_ai`, `wilderness`, `default_room`, the three `nomads_*` — and **35 of
vanilla's `ruler` rules were gone**: the fallen-empire set, the three hive
rooms, crisis, cosmogenesis, galactic emperor and custodian, marauder, khan, the
three specialist-subject rooms, the whole portrait-family block, and all four
pre-FTL eras.

And the Trek rooms it does name were unreachable anyway. All 29 gate on an STNH
country flag (`has_country_flag = bajoran_republic`) or an STNH scripted trigger
(`is_vulcan_story_empire`), and STG vendors neither — the same hole decisions 15
and 20 closed for clothing, one database later.

Net effect: of 327 room textures in the tree, the designer offered **19**.

Two live consequences, both found by the check written below and both
**playable empires**: the Tholian Assembly asked for `lithoid_room` and the Borg
Collective for `machine_room`, and STNH's `game_setup` offers neither.

## Nothing reported any of it, and the reason generalises

A room is the only art in Stellaris addressed by a **bare name with no path and
no declaration anywhere**. Vanilla's own comment says so:

> Script may point to textures in 'gfx\portraits\city_sets\' that end with
> "_room.dds" while ignoring the file ending, e.g.: "room = machine_room" will
> find 'machine_room(.dds)'.

Every cross-reference check in `tools/validate.py` asks whether a *declaration*
resolves. Nothing declares a room, so nothing could dangle, so the entire
database was invisible to twenty-odd checks at once. `check_vanilla_regression`
looked straight at the file and passed it: it compares depth-0 keys, and
`room_selector` was still there. Decision 31's lesson — *compare declared
identity, not block key* — one level deeper: here the identity is the **entries
inside** the block.

## Diverse Rooms ships a second `room_selector`, and that is unanswerable

`dr_room_textures.txt` declares `room_selector` too, with 302 `game_setup` rows
and 277 `ruler` rows. Vanilla declares that name exactly once in 172
asset-selector files, so vanilla cannot tell us what two files do, and the
question is not the usual FIOS/LIOS one about a *key* — it is whether a selector
merges at all.

Both readings are bad:

- **If it replaces**, the last filename wins, `room_textures.txt` beats
  `dr_room_textures.txt`, and Diverse Rooms — a source in the harvest on
  purpose — contributes 277 textures that nothing can ever select.
- **If it merges**, DR's ruler rows are `"barren1_room" = { exists = owner }`,
  277 of them with no other condition. An unconditional row in the `ruler` scope
  is not an offer, it is an override, and the first one would put every empire
  in the galaxy in a cave.

**So the ambiguity is removed rather than resolved.** DR's file is excluded in
`vendor.yml`; its 297 `game_setup` rows are merged into ours. Five are dropped —
`ethic_light_side*`, `ethic_dark_side*` — because they gate on a Star Wars mod's
ethics that nothing in the harvest declares, the same class as the UIOD
speeddial re-skins decision 43 excluded. `civic_imperial_cult` **is** vanilla, so
`imperium1/2` stay.

The three-file `room_selector` count is now one, and `check_room_references`
warns if it is ever more.

## What STG ships

`src/gfx/portraits/asset_selectors/room_textures.txt`, generated by
`tools/gen_room_selector.py` from vanilla + `.source/`:

- **vanilla 4.4 verbatim** as the base, so a game patch's additions arrive on the
  next run of the generator rather than being frozen into a hand-copy;
- **47 Trek rooms** — every `*_room.dds` STNH ships, read off the snapshot so the
  list cannot drift from the art — offered `always = yes`, as every vanilla row
  is. STNH's selector named only 29 of the 47; the other 18 (`deep_space9_room`,
  `klingon_bridge_room`, `starfleettng_room`, `galactic_council_room` …) were art
  it reaches from its own `common/`, and are now selectable;
- **20 `ruler` rows** keyed on species class, `owner = { owner_species = {
  is_species_class = KDF } }` — the idiom decision 15 proved. Placed after
  vanilla's situational overrides (crisis, galactic emperor, the origin- and
  flag-gated rooms) and before its portrait-family block, because a species look
  is the same kind of question as a lithoid look and should not outrank a crisis;
- **297 Diverse Rooms rows**, weights included, verbatim from its own file.

`futuresf_room` is not carried across: STNH's selector names it and no such
texture exists in either tree. The real file is `starfleetfuture_room.dds`,
which is offered.

## The per-empire assignment was a separate defect

STG's 101 prescripted empires were converted from STNH's, and
`room =` was not: 99 of 101 carried a vanilla `personality_*` room, so the 20
empires STNH gives a Trek room — the Federation, Vulcan, Klingons, Romulans,
Cardassians, Bajor, Ferengi, Andor, Bolians, Betazed, the Borg, the Dominion,
Suliban, Xindi, Sheliak, Voth, Undine, Terran Empire, Vau N'Akat, and the
Trill — lost it, and the 79 minors' personality picks did not match the source's
either.

This is decision 45's shape exactly, one field over: **a converted field that no
longer says what the source says, reported by nothing, because a room that
resolves to the wrong picture still resolves.**

`tools/fix_prescripted_rooms.py` restores STNH's own assignment, joining STNH's
`prescripted_countries/STH_*.txt` to ours by a CamelCase fold with 13 explicit
aliases for the empires STG renamed. **95 of 101 changed.** Three rows keep
STG's answer and the script names all three:

| empire | STNH says | STG ships | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trill Symbiosis | `personality_ruthless_capitalists_room` | `trillsym_room` | STNH's own *selector* maps its `trill_symbiosis_commission` country to `trillsym_room`; ours is the Symbiosis, not the Republic |
| Tholian Assembly | `personality_hive_mind_room` | `lithoid_room` | THO is LITHOID in STG (plan.md §6) and STNH ships no Tholian room |
| Borg Collective | `borg_room` | `borg_room` | listed because STG had `machine_room` and the **source was right** — not a divergence |

## The check

`check_room_references`, five questions, each with its vanilla ratio in the
docstring: two files claiming `room_selector`; a selector row naming a room with
no texture (0 of vanilla's 67, once rows whose whole body is `always = no` are
skipped — vanilla parks `synth_queen_room` that way and reading it as live is
what makes the ratio 1); a prescripted `room =` with no texture (0 of 29); a
**playable** prescripted `room =` the designer does not offer (0 of vanilla's 27
playable, against 2 of 29 unscoped — both misses are pre-FTL primitives, which
never reach the picker); and a `city_graphical_culture` with no
`<name>_city_l01.dds` (0 of 4), which rides here because it is the same bare-name
reference in the same file.

Calibrated by running it against the build *before* the repair: 4 findings, all
true — the two claimants, `futuresf_room`, and the Tholian and Borg rooms. 0
after.

## What is still open

Whether the engine consults the `ruler` scope for an empire whose `room =` was
set by its prescripted entry, or only for one that has none, is not established.
Both are covered — the 20 species rows and the 101 `room =` values agree — so
nothing depends on the answer, and it is recorded here rather than guessed at.
