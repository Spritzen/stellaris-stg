## Findings

### 1 — The last three slices of Phase 4 have never been in front of the game

**Severity: high, and it is a scheduling fact rather than a defect.**

`/paradox/stellaris/logs/error.log` was last written **2026-08-08 20:45**. The
anomalies landed at `cba8a81` (2026-08-09 09:15) and the dig sites and story
events at `272f3c7` (2026-08-09 13:31). So the baseline
[status.md](../planning/status.md) carries — 1,261 records, 1 record after the
startup window — describes a build that contained **none** of decisions 74–77's
work.

What that leaves unmeasured is the largest single addition the project has made:
21 anomaly categories, 6 dig sites, 23 story events, 144 sprites, 72 re-cut
pictures, 324 loc keys and ~11,200 words. `make validate` covers the references;
nothing covers the rest. Concretely still unknown:

- whether the 72 event pictures render at the right size in the popup — this is
  [decision 42](../decisions/42-event-picture-geometry.md)'s defect in
  [decision 74](../decisions/74-event-picture-families.md)'s new territory, and
  the 24 anomaly pictures are the ones with no vanilla control behind their crop;
- whether `stg_on_five_year_story_pulse` fires at all — a custom on_action
  reached only by `fire_on_action` is precisely the arrangement
  `check_story_events` was written to police, and
  [check design rule 7](../validation/check-design.md#7-compare-declared-identity-not-block-key--and-distrust-a-check-that-has-never-failed)
  says a check that has never failed in the live game is not yet evidence;
- whether any dig site is ever placed.

**The first live run after this audit should open the situation log and let a
survey fleet work**, and its `error.log` should be reconciled against the
2026-08-08 baseline group by group per [live-runs.md](../guides/live-runs.md).

### 3 — "21%" is the Federation's rate, not an empire's rate

*The comment and the rate are fixed; the content half — eleven empires with no story of their own — is deliberately left open and now named in [open-questions.md](../planning/open-questions.md). [Decision 78](../decisions/78-phase-4-count-corrections.md).*

**Severity: medium, and it is a content gap as much as a documentation one.**

`src/common/on_actions/stg_on_actions.txt:49` and
[decision 77](../decisions/77-trek-story-events.md) (line 74) both say "An STG empire
sees 2 of the 12 species-gated events at 60 and all eight open ones at 25 — 320
against `1200 = 0`, or 21%."

The arithmetic is exact. The premise is not: **only the Federation has two
species-gated events.** The 12 gated events cover 11 distinct species classes,
so the rate has three tiers:

| Empire | gated events in pool | weight | rate per eligible pulse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Federation (FED) | 2 | 320 | **21.1%** |
| the other 10 gated classes | 1 | 260 | **17.8%** |
| **11 of the 22 playable empires**, and all 79 AI minors | 0 | 200 | **14.3%** |

The eleven playable empires with **no institutional story of their own** are:
Bolian (BOL), Breen (BRE), Tholian (THO), Caitian (CAI), Xindi (XIN), Suliban
(SUL), Yridian (YRI), Krenim (KRE), Malon (MAL), Vidiian (VID) and the **mirror
Terran Empire (TER)**. The last is the notable one: it is a headline playable
empire from Phase 2, it has its own ENT-era uniforms from
[decision 64](../decisions/64-terran-empire-mirror-uniforms.md), and the story
pool has nothing for it.

[open-questions.md](../planning/open-questions.md) already asks "does the right
empire get the right story?" and names the two ways the *gate* could be wrong.
It does not name the third: half the playable roster is outside the gate
entirely. Growing the pool is cheap — `random_events` fires one winner, so
eleven more gated events cannot make anyone see more popups.

A second-order note on the same number: `stg_recent_story` runs 3,600 days
(9.86 years) and every story event sets it, so one fired event blanks the next
five-year pulse. The long-run frequency is therefore about `p / (1 + p)` per
pulse — ~17% for the Federation, ~13% for an ungated empire — rather than the
headline rate. That is a fine outcome, just not the one the comment describes.

### 4 — Nine anomaly categories are restricted, not seven

*Stated in the file's own header 2026-08-09; [decision 78](../decisions/78-phase-4-count-corrections.md).*

**Severity: low.** [Decision 75:87](../decisions/75-trek-anomalies.md) says
"Seven of the 21 are `max_once` or `max_once_global`." The file has **nine**:
five `max_once_global` (`planet_killer_remnant`, `iconian_gateway`, `tox_uthat`,
`deep_space_probe`, `omega_signature`) and four `max_once` (`resonator_cache`,
`living_ship`, `cetacean_probe`, `transwarp_conduit`).

The reasoning the decision gives is right and the file follows it — every
restricted category names a specific thing, every unrestricted one names a kind.
Only the tally is off. **43% restricted against vanilla's base-game 20%**, which
is a defensible consequence of a Trek anomaly set naming more specific objects,
but it is worth stating rather than under-reporting.

### 5 — Decision 76's headline finding is the one claim in Phase 4 with no check behind it

*Closed 2026-08-09 by [decision 79](../decisions/79-reachability-checks.md) — though **not in the ten-line form proposed below**: weight alone reports 74 of vanilla's 123, so the check asks the second half of the question too and needs no scope at all.*

**Severity: high, and this is the audit's most useful result.**

[Decision 76](../decisions/76-trek-archaeology.md) devotes its central section to
"**`weight` is the whole question, and six of vanilla's ten decline it**": a
`weight = 0` site is complete, correct, validating clean and unreachable, and
that is [decision 62](../decisions/62-city-set-cultures-undeclared.md)'s defect
class one database over.

`check_archaeology` does not ask it. Reading `tools/validate.py:4660–4725`, the
check asks about pictures, loc, `stages = N`, stage events, rune icons and
`RANDOM_EVENTS` — **the substring `weight` appears nowhere in it.** The same
asymmetry exists for `check_anomalies`, which never looks at `spawn_chance`.

All six sites and all 21 categories currently pass this question by hand — I
checked, 0 of 6 at `weight = 0`, 0 of 21 without a positive `spawn_chance`
modifier. The exposure is the next one somebody adds. `weight = 0` with
`#set via initialiser` is what six of vanilla's ten base-game sites look like,
so it is exactly what a seventh STG site copied from a vanilla template would
inherit, and it would ship silently.

This contradicts the project's own standing rule twice over —
[style guide §10](../style-guide.md#10-the-docs-get-the-same-treatment-the-mod-gets)
("a rule with no check behind it is a rule that erodes silently") and the
premise of `check_story_events`, which was written for precisely this defect
class in the on_actions database and *does* ask its version of the question.

**The check is roughly ten lines**: for each site in
`common/archaeological_site_types/`, if no `weight` block contains a positive
`add`/`base`, report it — scoped to STG's own file, because vanilla's floor
there is a known 6 of 10 and Planetary Diversity's is its own business. Same
shape for `spawn_chance` over `common/anomalies/`. Both are rule-11 scoped
questions of the kind the phase already writes three of.

## Smaller observations, offered rather than filed

**The anomaly difficulty curve is much steeper than vanilla's base game, and
nothing says so.** `level` gates which scientist can crack an anomaly and how
often the roll fails.

| level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STG, 21 categories | 2 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 6 | 3 | 0 | **3.8** |
| vanilla base game, 40 | 12 | 14 | 6 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 1 | **2.2** |

> **The two means are 3.86 and 2.4**, re-derived when this table was moved into
> [open-questions.md](../planning/open-questions.md) — vanilla's is 96 over 40.
> The distributions either side of them are right, and they are the part that
> carries the observation. [Decision 78](../decisions/78-phase-4-count-corrections.md).

Vanilla's base game puts 65% of its categories at level 1–2 and 10% at level 5+.
STG puts 19% at level 1–2 and **43% at level 5+**. The merged pool still leans
vanilla (348 categories), so the galaxy is not harder — but *the Trek half is
the slow, failure-prone half*, which is the half the player is meant to notice,
and early-game scientists will bounce off it. [open-questions.md](../planning/open-questions.md)
already lists "do the levels and rewards feel right" as eyes-only; this table is
the number to hold it against.

**The `clear_deposits` guard is applied by a rule the decision states more
strongly than vanilla practises.** [Decision 75](../decisions/75-trek-anomalies.md)
says vanilla "puts [it] before every research deposit it adds". Measured over
`anomaly_events_[0-9].txt`, vanilla guards **42 of 73** research-deposit adds —
but split by whether a permanent planet modifier is added alongside, it is
**24 of 29 with a modifier** and 6 of 11 without. STG guards 5 of 8, and the 5
are exactly the ones that also add a modifier. **The implementation follows
vanilla's real pattern; only the stated rule is absolute where vanilla is not.**

> **Re-derived for [decision 78](../decisions/78-phase-4-count-corrections.md):
> 40 of 69, split 29 of 42 with a modifier against 11 of 27 without** — the
> sub-totals above do not sum to the headline. The conclusion is unchanged and
> now sits in the events file's own header; STG's 5 of 8 verifies either way.

**`check_story_events`' hook-reachability question is one level deep.**
`stg_on_five_year_story_pulse` passes because `stg_story.2` textually contains
`fire_on_action = { on_action = stg_on_five_year_story_pulse }`. If `stg_story.2`
itself ever became unreachable, the hook would still read as fired. In practice
the sibling question ("a story event no on_action names") catches `stg_story.2`
today, so the pair is closed — it is a structural note, not a live hole.
